It flags the things people miss

Every draft is checked for the problems that catch assessors out, so they’re dealt with before an assessment is issued, not after an audit.

  • Out-of-date SDSOlder than three years, or still in the withdrawn DPD / R-phrase format.
  • SensitisersH317 and H334 substances that may need health surveillance.
  • Dust or vapour explosion riskA prompt to check your DSEAR assessment covers it.
  • CMR substances and inconsistenciesCarcinogens, mutagens and reproductive toxins, and limits in section 8 for ingredients missing from section 3.

Questions

Does it replace a competent assessor?

No. COSHH Studio drafts the assessment from the supplier’s SDS. A competent person checks it against how the substance is actually used in your workplace, edits anything that needs it, and signs it off. It helps you meet your duties under the COSHH Regulations 2002; it doesn’t replace them.

Where is my data kept?

Your assessments, sites and signatures are stored on your phone. The SDS you upload is sent securely to be read and the result is returned to the app. Read the privacy policy for the details.

What if it can’t read my SDS?

Scanned PDFs without text work best as photos: choose Photograph pages and take one photo per page in good light. If something still isn’t right, email us and we’ll look at it.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions › COSHH Studio and tap Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before it renews.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. It’s planned. Email hello@plant-safe.co.uk if you’d like to hear when it’s ready.
