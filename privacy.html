Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

COSHH Studio is built to collect as little as possible. Your assessments, sites and signatures stay on your phone.

Who we are

COSHH Studio is published by Kenneth Waugh (“we”), who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: hello@plant-safe.co.uk.

We don’t collect your name, email, location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites. There is no advertising in COSHH Studio.

On your phone only: your organisation name, sites, assessments, signatures and settings. We can’t see these. Deleting the app, or Delete all data on this device in Account, removes them.

AI processing

SDSs are read by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic PBC, which processes them on our behalf. You’re asked for permission in the app before your first upload. Anthropic does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models and keeps it only for a limited period under its commercial terms. Please don’t upload documents containing people’s personal details; SDSs normally contain none.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase – hosts our server and the extracted SDS data.
  • Anthropic – reads uploaded SDSs (see above).
  • RevenueCat – manages subscription status using your random app ID.
  • Apple – processes payments. We never see your payment details.

Some of these providers are based in, or use servers in, the United States. Where that happens, transfers are protected by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or equivalent safeguards. We never sell your information.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete information about you, object to processing, or ask us to restrict it. Email us with the app ID shown in Account and we’ll respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

COSHH Studio is a workplace tool and isn’t intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
